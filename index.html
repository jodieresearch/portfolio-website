About

A little about my work

Following my curiosity.

My PhD explored the history of women in football coaching, using oral history interviews alongside archival research to understand the barriers women faced and how they challenged them.

Speaking to people about their experiences, as well as digging through historical records, gave me a different perspective on a history that has often been overlooked. Alongside my doctoral research, I developed Coaching Tales, an interactive digital experience for the National Football Museum, bringing these histories to a wider audience.

More recently, I’ve been researching independent alternative provision costs in Sandwell, helping a provider benchmark their spending. Although quite different from my historical work, the project draws on the same skills: investigating a question, working with evidence and making sense of information to help inform decisions.

My varied experience has given me a broad range of transferable skills that I can bring to your project, whatever the subject or challenge.
