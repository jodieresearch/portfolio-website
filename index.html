About

A little about my work

Following my curiosity.

My PhD explored the history of women in football coaching, combining oral history interviews with archival research to understand the barriers women faced and how they challenged them.

Alongside my doctoral research, I developed Coaching Tales, an interactive digital experience for the National Football Museum, exploring how to bring overlooked histories to a wider audience.

More recently, I’ve researched independent alternative provision costs in Sandwell, helping a provider benchmark its spending. Different subjects, but a shared approach: ask the right questions, examine the evidence and make the findings useful.
