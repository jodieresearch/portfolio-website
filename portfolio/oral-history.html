03 / Oral history & qualitative research

Listening to
lived experience.

Oral history: women in football coaching

My doctoral research used oral history interviews alongside archival research to explore the barriers women faced in football coaching and how they challenged them.

Role

Doctoral research

Focus

Oral history interviews

Purpose

Understand experiences and barriers

01The question

What can personal testimony reveal?

How did women experience the barriers to football coaching, and how did they challenge them?

My PhD explored the history of women in football coaching, using oral history interviews alongside archival research.

Speaking to people about their experiences, as well as digging through historical records, gave me a different perspective on a history that has often been overlooked. Speaking with people about their experiences offered a way to examine a history that has often been overlooked and to consider individual accounts alongside the documentary record.

02The approach

Listening carefully. Interpreting in context.

Oral history combines attentive listening with careful interpretation of memories, experiences and the contexts in which they were formed.

01

Listen to experiences

Use interviews to understand how people describe their own experiences and perspectives.

02

Read across sources

Bring oral testimony into conversation with archival research and historical context.

03

Interpret themes

Examine recurring issues, differences and examples of how women responded to barriers.

03In practice

Making the themes visible.

A thematic diagram, a redacted research note or a permitted excerpt can help visitors see how interview material informed your analysis. Avoid publishing identifiable quotations without appropriate permission.

A considered approach

People's experiences are evidence.

Oral history can help illuminate how wider structures and changes were experienced in everyday life. It also requires care in how testimony is interpreted, represented and shared.

04Skills in practice

What this work demonstrates.

01

Interviewing and listening

Gathering detailed accounts and taking people's perspectives seriously.

02

Qualitative analysis

Interpreting testimony and exploring themes across different accounts.

03

Research ethics and context

Handling personal experiences thoughtfully and situating accounts within a wider historical framework.

05 / The value

Recovering a fuller picture of the past.

The research explored the barriers women faced in football coaching and the ways they challenged them.

It demonstrates how oral testimony and archival evidence can be used together to investigate complex questions about experience, opportunity and change.
