05 / Genealogy

Connecting the
family story.

Family history research

Tracing family histories through records, relationships and careful interpretation to build a clearer picture of the past.

Role

Genealogical research

Focus

Family records & relationships

Purpose

Build a coherent family history

01The question

How do separate records become a family story?

Names, dates and documents become meaningful when they are checked, connected and interpreted in context.

Family history research can begin with a handful of names or fragments of family knowledge and develop through the careful investigation of records.

Use this section to describe the specific family-history question, the starting information and what the research was intended to clarify. Do not include private details about living people.

02The approach

Building a picture, one source at a time.

A structured approach helps test assumptions, follow relationships and distinguish evidence from possibilities.

01

Establish what is known

Record the starting information and identify the questions that remain open.

02

Trace and cross-check records

Compare relevant documents and check that names, dates and relationships align.

03

Build the narrative

Bring verified details together into a clear timeline or family account, noting uncertainty where it remains.

03In practice

Show how the pieces connect.

A simple timeline, anonymised family-tree fragment or redacted record comparison can demonstrate your process without exposing sensitive personal information.

A considered approach

Careful research, handled sensitively.

Family history can involve personal and sometimes difficult histories. Clear source notes, careful verification and thoughtful decisions about what to share help make the final account both useful and respectful.

04Skills in practice

What this work demonstrates.

01

Record research

Finding and interpreting records relevant to a person's life and family relationships.

02

Verification and cross-checking

Comparing sources to resolve discrepancies and avoid relying on a single unconfirmed detail.

03

Clear storytelling

Turning separate pieces of evidence into an accessible account of family history.

05 / The value

A clearer account of family history.

Family-history research can help people understand relationships, trace life events and connect documentary records with the stories passed down through generations.
