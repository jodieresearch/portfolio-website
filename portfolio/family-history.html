The service

More than names and dates

Family history is about more than building a family tree. It’s about understanding the lives behind the names: where people lived, the work they did, the communities they belonged to, and the events that shaped their experiences.

I use reliable historical records, including collections available through Findmypast, to piece together your family’s history. I’ll explain the evidence in plain English, with sources and context to help you understand what the records reveal.

No jargon. No guesswork. Just carefully researched history, presented in a report you can keep and share.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Can’t I just do my family history research myself?

Yes, of course you can! Family history research can be fascinating, and there’s nothing stopping you from getting started.

But it can also be time-consuming. Records are spread across different websites, archives, and databases, and some require paid subscriptions or specialist knowledge to navigate.

With my service, you can leave the searching, cross-checking, and piecing together to me—and receive the findings in a clear, organised report. You get me to do the hard work for you.

What if you can’t find much information?

Not every family leaves the same paper trail. Some records may be missing, difficult to access, or contain conflicting information.

I’ll explain what I’ve found, what the evidence supports, and where the gaps remain. I won’t invent connections or make claims that the records can’t support.

Do you guarantee a certain number of generations?

The packages provide an indication of the research scope, but they aren’t a guarantee. How far a family line can be traced depends on the records available and the evidence they contain.

What information do you need from me?

The more you already know, the better the starting point. Names, approximate dates, places of birth or residence, and any family documents or stories can all help.

I’ll discuss what you have before beginning the research.

Do I need permission to research someone else’s family history?

If you’re commissioning research on behalf of someone else, please make sure they’re happy for their name and date of birth to be shared with me. These details can be needed to identify the correct records and trace family connections.

Historical records used in family history research are generally publicly accessible, but I’ll handle the information you provide carefully and use it only for the purposes of the agreed research.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit is required to begin research, with the remaining balance due when your report is delivered.

Any additional costs, such as archive fees or travel, will be discussed and agreed with you in advance.

How long will the research take?

The timeframe depends on the package, the complexity of the research, and the availability of records. I’ll provide an estimated timescale when we agree the scope of your project.

Can you research a particular ancestor or family mystery?

Absolutely. The advanced research package can be tailored around a particular person, family connection, or question. We’ll discuss what you’re hoping to find and whether the available records are likely to help.
