04 / Historical research

Finding stories
in the archive.

Archival work

Exploring documentary sources, assessing evidence and bringing records together to build a clearer account of the past.

Role

Historical research

Focus

Archives and documentary evidence

Purpose

Build evidence-led accounts

01The question

What can the records tell us?

Historical records rarely tell a complete story on their own. The task is to identify useful sources, understand their context and interpret them carefully.

Archival research involves finding and evaluating materials that can shed light on people, organisations, events and changes over time.

From scattered records to a coherent account.

A structured approach helps make the evidence traceable, interpretable and useful.

01

Assess the evidence

A considered approach

Evidence needs context.

Records reflect the circumstances in which they were created and preserved. Reading them alongside other sources helps reveal both what they tell us and what they leave out.
