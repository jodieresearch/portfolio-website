01 / Research & consultancy

Making sense
of costs.

Alternative provision cost benchmarking

Research into independent alternative provision in Sandwell, helping a provider benchmark its costs and better understand its spending.

My role

Research and analysis

Focus

Cost benchmarking

Purpose

Inform decision-making

01 The challenge

Understanding the costs behind provision.

How can research help a provider understand its spending in the context of a wider sector?

Understanding costs requires more than looking at individual figures. It means considering the information available, understanding its context and identifying where comparisons can offer useful insight.

This project focused on researching independent alternative provision and examining costs to help a provider develop a clearer understanding of its spending.

02 The approach

Research with a practical purpose.

A structured approach to investigating a question, examining evidence and making information useful.

01

Research the context

Investigate relevant information and build an understanding of the context in which costs need to be considered.

02

Examine the evidence

Analyse the available information and consider which comparisons can provide meaningful insight.

03

Interpret the findings

Connect the evidence to the original question and communicate what it can help the organisation understand.

03 Working with evidence

From information to insight.

Research is most useful when information is examined carefully and presented in a way that helps people understand its significance.

A considered approach

Understanding what the evidence can — and cannot — tell us.

Meaningful benchmarking depends on interpreting information in context, recognising the limits of comparisons and being clear about what the available evidence supports.

04 Skills in practice

Careful research. Practical insight.

01

Desk-based research

Finding and evaluating relevant information to investigate a specific question and establish its wider context.

02

Analytical thinking

Examining evidence, identifying useful comparisons and interpreting information rather than simply collecting it.

03

Evidence-led decision support

Connecting research to practical questions so that information can contribute to informed discussion and planning.

05 / The value

Making complex questions easier to navigate.

This project demonstrates how careful research and analysis can help organisations better understand costs, examine information in context and build an evidence base for decisions.

My approach combines curiosity and attention to detail with a practical focus on what the research can help people understand and do next.
