Understanding provider pricing and the financial context of alternative provision.

I researched the alternative provision market to help a client understand provider pricing and the financial context behind its pricing decisions.

Focus Market research
Methods Benchmarking & analysis

A note on confidentiality

This case study has been anonymised to respect client confidentiality. Client and provider names, locations and commercially sensitive details have been omitted. The focus is on the research, analysis and contribution I made.

01

The brief

Understanding the market before making pricing decisions.

My client wanted a better understanding of the alternative provision market and how its own pricing sat within it.

I brought together information spread across provider directories, published pricing information and company financial records.

02

The work

Building a picture from disparate sources.

I undertook desk research to identify providers and understand the range of provision available. I gathered published pricing information and organised it into a comparable format, allowing me to examine differences across providers and provision types.

I also reviewed publicly available company financial information to add a commercial perspective to the research.

My contribution

01

Mapped the provider landscape

Used directories and publicly available information to identify providers and understand the range of provision.

02

Collected and benchmarked pricing

Gathered and compared published prices to explore variation across the market.

03

Reviewed company financials

Examined publicly available financial information to add context to the market research.

04

Synthesised the findings

Brought the research together to inform my client's understanding of the market and its pricing decisions.

03

The value

Connecting research with commercial thinking.

The project brought together market and financial information to support a more informed view of the market.

For me, it drew on the areas of work I most enjoy: understanding a new context and translating research into relevant insight.

What the project developed

Bringing business, strategy and financial insight together.

This project strengthened my ability to connect business research with strategic thinking and financial insight, translating complex market data into a clearer understanding of commercial opportunities and challenges.
